Best Download Manager

Privacy Policy

Effective 13 October 2026 · updated 29 October 2026 (auto-groups and cleanup, emails you send us) · applies to the Best Download Manager (“BDM”) Chrome extension

In short: BDM works entirely on your device. It does not collect, send, sell or share any of your data. There are no accounts, no analytics and no BDM servers involved in using the extension.

What BDM accesses

  • Your Chrome download history (through Chrome's Downloads API): file names, folder paths, sizes, file types, download dates, download status and the web address a file came from. BDM reads this only to show, search, sort and organize your downloads inside the extension.
  • Files themselves: BDM never reads, uploads, moves, copies or deletes your files. “Open” and “Show in folder” ask Chrome to do this for you. “Remove from History” removes only Chrome's history entry; the file stays on your computer.

What BDM stores

Using Chrome's local extension storage, on your device only: your settings (theme, layout and alert preferences), your collections (their names and the IDs of the downloads you added), your auto-group rules and saved views, and small counters used to limit tips and notifications. Nothing is synced or sent elsewhere by BDM.

Exporting and importing collections

When you choose Export collections, BDM saves a JSON file in your Downloads folder. It contains your collection names and, for each file in them, the file's name, size, the website's hostname and the download and added dates. It does not include file locations on your computer or full web addresses. BDM never sends this file anywhere; where you keep or share it is up to you. When you choose Import collections, BDM reads the file you select on your computer to re-link its entries to downloads in your browser's history. Nothing is uploaded.

Auto-groups and cleanup

Auto-groups are rules you write (for example “extension is .csv”). BDM applies them on your device to your download history; the rules and which files you deleted are saved in local extension storage and never leave your device. BDM can show a notification, at most once a day, when files in a group are ready to delete. BDM never deletes a file on its own: a file is deleted from your computer only after you select it, tap Delete and confirm.

What BDM does not do

  • No analytics, tracking, advertising or crash-reporting services.
  • No sale or transfer of data to anyone, and no use of data for purposes unrelated to managing your downloads.
  • No use of data to determine creditworthiness or for lending.

Removing your data

Use Settings → Reset local data in BDM to clear its settings and collections, or remove the extension from Chrome to delete everything it stored. Your files and Chrome's own download history are not affected.

Emails you send us

If you email us — for support, or to join the BDM for Mac waitlist — we use your address only to reply, or to tell you once when BDM for Mac launches. It is never shared or sold. Ask us to remove it at any time and we will delete it. The extension itself never sends your email address or any other data anywhere.
